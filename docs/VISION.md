# Vision

## Core Idea

A silly 2D physics delivery game where a frog rides a pizza moped across rough terrain while trying to keep a stack of pizza boxes from flying out.

The twist is the frog tongue: fallen pizzas can be grabbed and swung back toward the delivery caddy like a loose grappling rope.

## Design Pillars

## Physics First

The fun should come from wobble, recovery, mistakes, and surprising physical interactions.

## Flash-Era Driving Feel

Driving should feel responsive and readable, closer to old motorcycle physics games than strict simulation.

## Recoverable Chaos

Pizzas should fall out sometimes, but the player should often feel they can save the run with skillful tongue control.

## Silly, Clear, Primitive

Everything uses simple shapes and colors. No external assets are needed for this prototype.

## Prototype Over Polish

The purpose is to answer whether the mechanic is fun. Visual polish, menus, progression, and content variety come later.
